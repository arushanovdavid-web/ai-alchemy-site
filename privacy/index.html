Privacy

Effective: November 15, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you share with us through this website.

This website is run by AI Alchemy, a technology consultancy based in the Greater Toronto Area. You can reach us at hello@ai-alchemy.ca.

Your project brief stays with you until you send it

The brief builder on our contact page runs in your browser. What you type stays on your device while you fill it in. It isn’t sent to us.

When you choose “Send by email”, your own email app opens with the brief inside. The brief isn’t sent until you press send in that app.

If you choose “Copy brief”, the text goes to your device’s clipboard. It’s yours to paste wherever you like.

If you close the page without sending, we don’t see what you wrote.

When you email us

Your brief, or any email you write to us, goes to hello@ai-alchemy.ca. It includes whatever you chose to share: usually your name, your company, your email address and what you’d like to improve.

We use it only to reply to you. We don’t use it for marketing, and we don’t share it with anyone.

Email sent to hello@ai-alchemy.ca passes through ImprovMX, an email forwarding service, on its way to us.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and briefs for 24 months. To have yours deleted sooner, email hello@ai-alchemy.ca and we’ll delete it.

No analytics, no cookies

This website doesn’t use analytics and doesn’t set cookies. We don’t track your visit.

Where this website runs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, as part of running the service.

The pages don’t load anything from other services. Our fonts are served from this website.

Questions or requests

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@ai-alchemy.ca and we’ll take care of it.

If this page changes, we’ll update the effective date at the top.
