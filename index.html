Sheet CWho we work with

Built for established businesses with complex operations.

We work with mid-market organizations across the Greater Toronto Area. We focus on the industries below, but we don’t limit our work to them.

Each industry has its own problems. The need underneath is often the same: reliable systems, clear processes, and information that moves.

  • Manufacturing, Wholesale & LogisticsQuotes, stock and shipments sit in spreadsheets, email and portals. That can mean wrong quotes, backorders and no live status.
  • Mortgage & InsuranceDocuments, deadlines and renewals are tracked by hand across older systems. Closings can run late, and renewals can slip.
  • HVAC, Energy & Home ImprovementSchedules, onsite estimates and service contracts live on paper or in unlinked apps. Dispatch can lose time, and maintenance visits can slip.
  • Financial, Professional Services & Non-ProfitsClient and donor records are split across teams and improvised tools. Onboarding, follow-up and grant deadlines are hard to keep straight.
  • Your industry isn’t listed? If your operation runs on scattered tools and manual handoffs, send us a brief and we’ll tell you honestly whether we’re a fit.
