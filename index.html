INDUSTRIES WE SERVE

The work looks different. The need is the same.

Connect the records, handoffs, and updates that slow teams down.

Make, stock, and ship

Manufacturing, including glass fabricators. Wholesale, distribution, and logistics.

Problem

Quote, stock, and shipment details sit in spreadsheets, separate tools, email, and portals. That leads to bad quotes, backorders, and no live status.

Fix

Connect sales, inventory, the shop floor, and freight in one system. Turn approved quotes into jobs, and send customers milestone updates.

Keep closings and renewals on track

Mortgage brokerages and insurance.

Problem

Documents, lender or underwriting rules, deadlines, and renewals are tracked by hand on separate older systems. Applicants drop off, closings run late, renewals get missed, and claims move slowly.

Fix

Bring intake, document collection, renewals, and claims tracking into one place. Send status updates at each milestone.

Keep crews and visits on schedule

HVAC, energy, and home improvement.

Problem

Scheduling technicians, onsite estimates, and service contracts live on paper or in unlinked apps. Dispatch time is lost, and maintenance visits are missed.

Fix

Connect dispatch, field estimates, and maintenance schedules. Link service records to client accounts and billing.

One record for clients and donors

Financial and professional services, and non-profits.

Problem

Client and donor records are split across teams and improvised tools. That makes onboarding, compliance, follow-up, donor care, and grant deadlines hard to keep straight.

Fix

Keep each client's or donor's information in one place. Track onboarding, communication, review milestones, and grant deadlines alongside it.
