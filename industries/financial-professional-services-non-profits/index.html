The workflow: records, onboarding, then deadlines. A simple financial, professional services and non-profit workflow: records in one place, onboarding and communication, then deadlines.

Problem and fix

Where the work gets stuck, and how we connect it.

Records split across teams

Problem

Client and donor records are split across teams and improvised tools. The whole picture is hard to see.

Fix

Keep each client’s or donor’s information in one place.

Onboarding and follow-up that slip

Problem

Onboarding and follow-up are hard to keep straight when the details sit in different tools.

Fix

Track onboarding and communication beside each client’s or donor’s record.

Deadlines that are hard to keep straight

Problem

Grant deadlines sit in separate tools from the records they belong to.

Fix

Track deadlines alongside the record, where the people responsible can see them.
