Problem and fix

Where the work gets stuck, and how we connect it.

Quotes built from scattered details

Problem

Quote details sit in spreadsheets, email and portals. Pulling them together by hand is how quotes go wrong.

Fix

Connect sales, inventory and the shop floor in one system, so quotes aren’t built from scattered copies.

Stock that doesn’t match the orders

Problem

Stock details sit in spreadsheets and portals, away from the orders that depend on them. That leads to backorders.

Fix

Keep inventory and orders in one system. Enter information once, so stock and orders are easier to keep in step.

Shipments with no live status

Problem

Shipment details sit in portals and email threads. There’s no live status for your team or your customers.

Fix

Connect freight to the rest of the operation. Send customers milestone updates.

Outcomes depend on your systems, data and processes. We agree goals with you before work starts.
