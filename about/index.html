We start by understanding your business

AI Alchemy is an architecture-first technology consulting and engineering company. Our team is based in the Greater Toronto Area and does the work itself. Your first conversation is with David Arushanov, our Client Solutions Lead.

One team, from strategy through build.

01What we believe

Technology should fit the business. Not the other way around.

  • Start with how the business works

    Projects shouldn’t start with a feature list.

  • The answer isn’t always new software

    Sometimes it’s to improve a system you already have. Sometimes it’s to connect applications that already work. Sometimes the business needs something built for it.

  • You keep approval

    In the systems we build, approval follows your organization’s structure. The people you name decide on technical, pricing, safety and production actions.
