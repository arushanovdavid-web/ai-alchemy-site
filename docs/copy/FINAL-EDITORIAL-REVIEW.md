## Checked and deliberately left alone

- **Facts that are consistent across pages.** "We reply within one business day" says the same thing on every page and in `llms.txt`. The free first conversation is consistent on home, Contact and the `/services/` pricing answer. David Arushanov is "our Client Solutions Lead" on home, About and Contact. The brief is consistently described as opening as an email the visitor sends, and nothing implies a form submission or a backend. The privacy, no-analytics and no-cookies statements match.
- **Chapter 6 H2 repeats the hero sentence and the footer tagline.** "We bring disconnected technology together into systems that work" appears three times on the homepage. The H2 is locked, and the repeat works as a deliberate bookend, so I made no change.
- **The hero does not name Systems Integration & Modernization.** The first sentence ("bring disconnected technology together"), the secondary link ("See how we connect systems") and the chapter 1 link carry the lead offering. Adding the name would make a hero that is already long on mobile even longer.
- **"Then the same team builds it."** This appears on home and `/services/` and is supported by "Built in-house. No outsourcing."
- **"Products & Innovation" band and the "Taking shape" stamp.** These match the locked owner line. Nothing is named or implied to be available.
- **Industries index: "The four above are where we focus."** This is compatible with the homepage line "we don't limit our work to them", so it is not a contradiction.
- **Manufacturing "Send customers milestone updates." and Mortgage "Send status updates at each milestone."** Both are already live on `main`, so they are approved copy.
- **About: "One team, from strategy through build."** It differs slightly from "strategy to go-live" elsewhere. Not material.
- **Enterprise AI step 4: "Approval follows your organization's structure."** This still fits the locked reassurance. Align it later if the owner wants one wording everywhere.
- **Contact: "Email reaches us directly."** This is live copy. The privacy page discloses that mail passes through ImprovMX for forwarding. "Directly" can be read as no form and no intermediary staff, so I left it. The owner may prefer "Email comes straight to our team."
- **No leftover old positioning in the pages.** Neither "consultancy" nor "implementation firm" appears anywhere. The only exceptions are the bare "Technology consulting" in the About title and the `/industries/` description (rows 8 and 11).
