The problem

Software that works separately.

Your software works separately, leaving your people to move information between tools and departments.

Information gets trapped in separate systems. Teams repeat work that should happen once, and each new tool adds one more place to look.

What we do

We get your systems working together.

  • Connect the applications you already use

    We link your CRM, ERP, accounting and operations tools, so information entered in one reaches the others.

  • Move to a new system without losing what you have

    When you replace a system, such as a CRM, we plan the migration so your records and history come with you.

  • Build the connections you can’t buy

    When two systems have no ready-made connector, we build one around how your teams pass work between them.

  • Keep what already works

    Sometimes the answer is to connect applications that already work, not to replace them.

How we work

Understand your business. Build what moves it forward.

  1. Step 1 · Understand

    Understand the people, systems and bottlenecks.

    We learn how your business runs today: who does the work, which systems they use, and where information gets stuck.

  2. Step 2 · Design

    Design the right solution.

    You see the plan and the trade-offs before anything is built. Sometimes that means improving a system you already have, connecting what already works, or building something new.

  3. Step 3 · Build

    Build, test and support adoption.

    We build in stages and connect to what already works. Your team tests it on real scenarios and approves before anything goes live, and we help your people start using it.

Outcomes depend on your systems, data and processes. We agree goals with you before work starts.
