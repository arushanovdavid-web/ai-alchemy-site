What we solve

Architecture first. Then the build.

We design how your systems should fit together before anyone writes code. Then the same team builds it.

  • Disconnected systems and workflows

    We connect the applications you already use, so information is entered once and reaches the person who handles it next.

  • CRM and ERP integrations and migrations

    When customer records sit in one system and orders sit in another, we connect them or move you to one that fits, in stages.

  • Legacy modernization

    When older software holds the records but not the way you work now, we bring it up to date and connect it to the rest of the business.

  • Custom software and engineering

    When nothing off the shelf fits, we build software around your real operations.

  • AI automation and intelligent workflows

    We find the steps your teams repeat and add AI-assisted steps where they fit, with people keeping oversight.

  • Technology architecture and planning

    Before you commit to development, we assess your systems, find the gaps and set the order of work against your business priorities.

Where to start

Not sure which service fits?

You don’t need to know. Sometimes the answer is to improve a system you already have. Sometimes it’s to connect applications that already work. Sometimes the business needs something built for it. The brief helps us tell which.

How is a project priced?

At a fixed price or by the hour, depending on the scope. Once we understand the work, we propose the model that fits and tell you why: a fixed price when the scope is clear, an hourly rate while it’s still being worked out. The first conversation is free.
